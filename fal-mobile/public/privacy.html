Effective: [[YURURLUK_TARIHI]] · English version of the Turkish policy, which governs in case of conflict.

Telve is a coffee-cup reading, tarot, natal chart and dream interpretation app. This page explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how long we keep it.

In short: no email, password or phone number is required. Server copies of cup photos are deleted as soon as the reading is generated; a device-only copy may be kept for up to 30 days so the visual overlay works. You can permanently delete everything from inside the app.

2. No account required

There is no sign-up screen. On first launch the app generates a random identifier on your device and your data is linked to it. This is not an advertising identifier, is not shared with other apps, and does not identify you personally. Reinstalling the app creates a new identifier; previous data becomes unreachable.

4. Your cup photo

The server copy is never written to permanent storage. It is held in a temporary store with a 24-hour maximum lifetime and deleted as soon as the reading finishes. To keep visual markers available in reading history, the app stores a copy only in its private area on your device for up to 30 days and no more than 20 photos. Account deletion removes these local copies. Small crops of the detected coffee-ground regions are sent to our AI provider to identify what the shape resembles (see section 5). The full photo is not sent.

6. Age

Telve is intended for users aged 18 and over. The app does not generate relationship or fate readings for users who indicate they are under 18. If we learn we have processed data belonging to someone under 18, we delete it.

7. Safety and crisis handling

If your text contains a serious crisis signal (self-harm or suicidal ideation), the app does not generate a reading. It shows emergency helpline numbers for your country instead. This check runs on our server before generation, and that text is not sent to the AI provider.

This is not a health service. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

8. Your rights

You can ask whether we process your data, what we use it for, who we share it with, and request correction or deletion. You can object to results produced by automated analysis and seek compensation for unlawful processing.

Fastest route: in the app, Profile → Delete my data. Your birth details, reading history, prediction ledger and memory records are permanently deleted within 24 hours. This cannot be undone. See the data deletion page.

For anything else, email [[KVKK_EPOSTA]]. We respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies

The web version uses your browser's local storage to remember your session and language. We use no advertising or tracking cookies. These legal pages contain no cookies or trackers at all.

10. Changes

If we update this policy we change the effective date, and we notify you in the app for significant changes.
